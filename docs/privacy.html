Grappling Dex privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version

Grappling Dex does not collect, send or sell any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores

The sessions you log, the moves in them and any starting stages you set are saved in a database on your phone. They never leave the phone unless you make a backup.

Backups

When you tap Back up, the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as Files, a cloud drive or an email, and that service's own privacy terms apply. Restoring reads a file you pick and replaces the progress on the phone.

Deleting your data

Remove a session from the Progress tab, or delete the app to erase everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version adds accounts or syncing, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app.

Contact

Questions go to the project's GitHub page.
